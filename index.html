Prism

Sort, filter and rename the streams of any Stremio addon.

Shown in Stremio's addon list and at the start of every stream name.

Qualities to keep

Unchecked qualities are hidden. Others covers streams with no detected resolution.

Separate with commas. Kept qualities you leave out go last, highest first.

Order by file size within the same quality

Streams with no known size go last in their quality.
